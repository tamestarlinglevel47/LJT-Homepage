## About Me
I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with core interests in LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning, Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), and LLM truthfulness and Interpretability.

## Academic Background
- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present)**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), affiliated with HKUST NLP Group
- **B.Eng. (2020-2024)**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduated in June 2024, and I received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship during my undergraduate study.

## Research Experience
- **Research Intern (February 2025 - Present)**, MINIMAX (AI/technology company), continuing my research work on large language models and related topics.
- **Research Intern (June 2024 - September 2024)**, Tencent WXG, advised by Zifei Shan, conducted research related to vision-language models and model perception.
- **Research Intern (June 2023 - December 2023)**, Shanghai AI Lab, advised by Prof. Yu Cheng, focused on research related to LLM truthfulness and interpretability.
