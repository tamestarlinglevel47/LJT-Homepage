In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation, published at ICML 2024, Junteng Liu is co-author. This work proposes a novel inner representation perspective to mitigate hallucinations in large language models using in-context sharpness as alerts.
